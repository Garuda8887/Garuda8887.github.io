About

CS graduate with a background in behavioural science — including addiction psychiatry research at Harvard Medical School. I build privacy-first tools that are designed to actually be usable, not just by developers, but by anyone.

I believe the best software is invisible — it solves the problem without asking the user to understand how it works. That philosophy shows up in everything I build: clean interfaces, self-hosted by default, no unnecessary complexity. Deep into defensive cybersecurity, cognitive science applied to learning, and keeping data where it belongs — on your own hardware.

I build for people first, developers second.
